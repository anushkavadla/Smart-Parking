## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a premium, cheerful, miniature 3D-world aesthetic tailored for smart parking navigation, space reservation, and connected vehicle management. It merges the precision of modern mobility software with the tactile charm of an isometric diorama: crisp floating cards, pill-shaped markers, soft ambient light, and luminous micro-indicators.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Response
- **Approachable & Optimistic:** Parking is traditionally stressful; this interface counters friction with warm tones, tactile depth, and gentle curves that feel welcoming rather than bureaucratic.
- **Architectural & Precise:** Clear slot geometry, isometric visual grounding, and high-readability typographic hierarchies deliver effortless utility during quick glance-and-go interactions.
- **Toy-box Elegance:** Elevated, creamy base surfaces and pristine floating panels mimic physical model sets, fostering delightful spatial awareness without clutter.

### Design Movement & Influence
- **Tactile Soft-Modernism:** Pillowy contours, subtle warm under-shadows, and layered floating white panels over an architectural warm ivory ground.
- **Isometric Micro-World Visuals:** Interfaces sit as floating HUD controllers overlaying clean 3D diorama viewports with soft ambient occlusion and crisp focal clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an open, sunny world reminiscent of architectural scale models, balanced by high-contrast legible typography and functional spatial semantics.

### Roles & Application
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F5EF`):** Warm ivory/cream foundation that removes sterile display glare and creates an organic, paper-like warmth for the isometric world.
- **Surfaces & Overlays (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container panels that float cleanly above the ivory canvas with warm ambient drops.
- **Primary Interactive (`#1677FF` - Sky Blue):** Navigation routes, active vehicle selection, system links, and focused input indicators.
- **Action & Checkout Accent (`#FF8A3D` - Warm Vibrant Orange):** High-priority call-to-actions, spot reservation triggers, payment confirmation, and live session timers.
- **Anchor Neutral (`#0F3D6E` - Rich Navy):** Deep supportive accent used for headers, secondary badges, telemetry metrics, and structural sidebars.
- **Base Typography (`#172033` - Deep Charcoal):** Primary text color delivering WCAG AAA contrast against white cards and ivory canvas.

### Spatial Semantic Accents
- **Available Slot (`#35C98A` - Mint Green):** Unoccupied bays, charging readiness, and successful check-in states.
- **Occupied / Reserved Slot (`#FF6B6B` - Soft Coral):** Active occupancy, no-parking zones, and critical alerts.
- **Specialty / Valet (`#8B7CFF` - Subtle Lavender):** EV Superchargers, reserved accessibility slots, car-wash bays, and premium membership tiers.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances characterful geometry with high-utility readability:
- **Headings & Badges (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Rounded terminal geometry and wide apertures give the UI a warm, forward-leaning posture that echoes the friendly curves of car silhouettes and road navigation iconography.
- **Body & Data Metrics (Manrope):** Geometric yet neutral, Manrope supplies exceptional numeric tabular clarity for spot IDs, pricing counters, battery percentages, and timestamps.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- Numerical indicators (e.g., "$4.50/hr", "Bay 42B") should use `label-lg` or `headline-md` rendered with tabular lining figures.
- Micro-status labels (e.g., "AVAILABLE", "CHARGING") rely on uppercase `label-sm` with widened letter-spacing (+0.04em) to maintain crisp legibility at compact miniature scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The viewport operates on a dynamic viewport-first paradigm: a central canvas displaying the 3D parking diorama bounded by non-blocking floating heads-up display (HUD) panels and drawers.

### Grid & Canvas Behavior
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Full-bleed 3D viewport canvas. UI controllers anchor within an off-canvas left floating navigation bar (width: 320px) and a contextual right booking tray (width: 380px) with `space-lg` internal margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Collapsible sidebar rail; bottom modal sheet for slot booking details with `gutter-sm` spacing between quick-action controls.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Top floating pill HUD (search + vehicle switch) with an interactive draggable bottom sheet (peek height: 110px, expanded: 85vh) maximizing unobstructed view of the miniature level map.

### Internal Spatial Rhythm
- Compact HUD indicators and pill buttons use `space-xs` and `space-sm` internal padding to preserve miniature visual fidelity.
- Interactive slot booking cards utilize `space-md` inner padding with `space-lg` separation between form stages.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy reflects physical model architecture: components sit on crisp, pillowed white plates casting warm, diffused shadows onto the cream floor.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Deck (Level 0):** `#F7F5EF` ivory floor. Contains zero shadow; acts as the foundational plane for the 3D canvas and road lanes.
- **Stationary Trays & Cards (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle border `1px solid rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.06)` and warm ambient drop: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.03)`.
- **Floating HUD & Overlays (Level 2):** Floating search pills, vehicle selectors, and interactive spot popovers: `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.04)`.
- **Active Selection & Modals (Level 3):** Spot checkout drawers and confirmation popups: `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(15, 61, 110, 0.14), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.06)`.

### Tactile Detail
Buttons and active parking tiles utilize a subtle inset highlight (`inset 0px 1px 0px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`) to create a satisfying, resin-molded physical presence.

## Shapes

The shape system employs friendly, ergonomic geometry that echoes scaled die-cast toys and smooth industrial transport design.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Standard UI Containers & Cards:** 1rem (`rounded-lg`) curvature provides a soft visual frame without sacrificing data density.
- **Modals & Master Sheets:** 1.5rem (`rounded-xl`) outer radius gives a soft, welcoming feel to large surfaces.
- **Badges, Status Tags, and Map Pins:** Full pill curvature (`9999px`) provides clear contrast against rectangular parking space grids.
- **Parking Bay Outlines:** 0.5rem corner smoothing matches real-world painted curb geometry scaled to miniature proportions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Warm Orange):** Background `#FF8A3D`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded-lg` or pill. Hover: `#F07826` with slight vertical lift (`transform: translateY(-1px)`). Inset top highlight gives tactile depth.
- **Secondary Action (Sky Blue):** Background `#1677FF`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for "Find Route", "Auto-Park", and navigation functions.
- **Ghost / Neutral Button:** Background `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.12)`, text `#0F3D6E`. Hover background: `#F7F5EF`.

### Parking Slot Chips & Status Badges
- **Available Spot Chip:** Pill shape. Background `rgba(53, 201, 138, 0.12)`, text `#1F8A5D`, border `1px solid rgba(53, 201, 138, 0.3)`. Includes an animated pulsing mint dot indicator.
- **Occupied Spot Chip:** Pill shape. Background `rgba(255, 107, 107, 0.12)`, text `#D93838`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 107, 107, 0.25)`.
- **EV Supercharger Chip:** Pill shape. Background `rgba(139, 124, 255, 0.14)`, text `#5F4FE3`, border `1px solid rgba(139, 124, 255, 0.3)`.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Floating pill-form bar with `#FFFFFF` background, deep charcoal text, and an orange or sky-blue focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.2)`).
- Prefix icons (search loupe, garage pin, license plate) rendered in `#0F3D6E`.

### Cards & Drawers
- Spot reservation card: White panel with rounded-lg edges. Features vehicle avatar preview, distance tag, walk-time metric, and bold orange checkout action button anchored at the bottom.
- Level Switcher Widget: Vertical floating pill container with stacked numbered badges representing parking floors (G, P1, P2, P3).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded circular checkboxes (radius: `0.375rem`), with mint green or sky blue fill upon selection, featuring a tactile white check icon.
